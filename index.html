About

I am a systems software engineer working where hardware, robotics, and large models meet. Across research prototypes and production platforms, I turn ambiguous technical problems into measurable systems, reproducible workflows, and tools that developers can trust. At NVIDIA, that means pushing new AI and robotics platforms until their limits become clear.

Day to day that means cross-platform benchmarking, deep functional and quality reviews, performance tuning of LLM/VLM inference, and building agent-driven automation that turns multi-week evaluations into days. I also build demos and reviewer collateral for launches like DGX Spark and Jetson, teach hands-on DLI courses, and represent the stack to reviewers and press.

Before the benchmarking and evaluation work, I contributed to NVIDIA's GPU-accelerated robotics stack — motion planning and ROS 2 manipulation on Jetson. I'm happiest turning messy, ambiguous systems into reproducible, well-documented results.
